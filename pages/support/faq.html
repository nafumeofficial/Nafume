Fragrance Care & Performance

Keep your NAFUME vessels away from heat, high-humidity areas (like bathrooms), and direct sunlight. Store them inside dry, cool cabinets or drawers. This preserves the delicate top notes of saffron and citrus orchards from oxidizing.

High-maturation botanical concentrations (like real Mysore Sandalwood and aged Oud) continue to mature and maturate inside the glass vessel over months. Exposure to oxygen slightly blooms the heavier resins, deepening sillage and longevity.
